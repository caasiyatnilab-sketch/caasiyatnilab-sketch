## 2025-05-14 - Skip Links and Focus Management
**Learning:** Adding a 'Skip to Content' link and managing programmatic focus after smooth scrolling significantly improves accessibility for keyboard and screen reader users. Sections must have 'tabindex="-1"' to be focusable via script.
**Action:** Always include a skip-to-content link as the first child of '<body>' and ensure navigation links manage focus to their targets.
